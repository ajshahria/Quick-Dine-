Status: NOT VERIFIED. Do not replace this with "genuine" without a factual basis.

The 18 October audit found three identities matching committed seed accounts,
linked to three of five older bookings. The remaining records have unknown

or adoption. QA workflow records are separately excluded.

To support a genuine-data evaluation, the data owner should record:

| Item | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Source | Actual restaurant operation or identified, consented participant study |
| Collection period | Start/end dates and business timezone |
| Genuine subset | Internal record identifiers or an anonymized inclusion manifest kept privately |
| Exclusions | Seed accounts, developer trials, QA bookings and load traffic |
| Consent/access basis | Who authorized collecting and analyzing the records |
| Measures | Genuine users/restaurants/bookings; completed/cancelled counts; capacity checks |
| Data owner attestation | Who verified these statements and when |

Do not put passwords, database URLs, real customer contacts, or participant names
in a public report. Counts and anonymized aggregates are sufficient for the
published evaluation; the private inclusion manifest should stay private.

Until this record is completed, report the existing measurements as deployed
application-data tests with mixed/unknown provenance. A synthetic workflow can
validate software behavior but does not create genuine customer evidence.
